ABOUT NEW POINT

OUR BACKGROUND

New Point Masonry & Construction LLC is a Greater Houston masonry contractor serving commercial, public, and residential projects throughout the Texas Triangle. Our work includes structural masonry, architectural wall finishes, selective masonry demolition, and custom cast stone fabrication. Behind the company is a practical understanding of both construction planning and field execution. We approach each project with attention to its requirements, the conditions on site, and the coordination needed to complete our work.

New Point is owned and operated by Alvaro, whose background combines construction field experience with a Bachelor of Science in Construction Management. Experience in different construction roles has shaped his understanding of how estimating, scheduling, coordination, and workmanship affect a project’s outcome.

That background informs the way New Point approaches its work: reviewing the details, asking questions early, and communicating with the project team as work progresses.

OUR APPROACH

Safety, quality, and integrity guide how we work. To us, that means planning carefully, taking pride in the finished product, and being straightforward about our scope and responsibilities.

We value clear expectations and practical coordination—from the initial proposal through completion. Our goal is to build lasting working relationships through dependable service and attention to the details that matter to our clients.
