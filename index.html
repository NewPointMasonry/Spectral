WHO WE ARE

SAFETY, QUALITY, INTEGRITY

Our mission is to provide dependable masonry services that meet the demands of each project’s scope, schedule, and budget without compromising safety or workmanship. Based in the Greater Houston area, New Point Masonry serves general contractors, developers, property owners, and public and private organizations throughout the Texas Triangle.

Our capabilities include new structural masonry construction, selective masonry demolition, structural wall modifications, and exterior wall systems featuring brick, stone, stucco, and EIFS.

With in-house cast stone fabrication capabilities, we deliver greater flexibility over design, production, and scheduling.
